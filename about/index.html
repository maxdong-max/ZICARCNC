About ZICARCNC

Providing Cost-Effective CNC Products And Solutions

ZICARCNC (Guangzhou Santuo Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd.) is a professional woodworking machinery manufacturer with over 30 years of experience in CNC solutions. We specialize in six-sided drilling machines, five-sided drilling machines, and intelligent CNC production lines.

Our products are trusted by leading furniture manufacturers worldwide, including Quanyou, Suofeiya, Holike, and other top home enterprises. We are committed to providing cost-effective CNC products and solutions for the furniture manufacturing industry.
