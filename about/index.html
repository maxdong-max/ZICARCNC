About ZICARCNC

Leading manufacturer of professional woodworking machinery with over 15 years of experience.

Excellence in CNC Manufacturing

ZICARCNC is a leading manufacturer of professional woodworking machinery, specializing in intelligent CNC solutions for the furniture industry. With over 15 years of experience, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for major furniture manufacturers worldwide.

Our products integrate advanced technology with practical design, helping customers achieve higher productivity and better quality in their manufacturing processes. We serve renowned companies including Quanyou, Suofeiya, Holike, and other industry leaders.

Our commitment to innovation has led to the development of industry-leading solutions that transform how furniture is manufactured. We continuously invest in R&D to bring cutting-edge technology to our customers.
