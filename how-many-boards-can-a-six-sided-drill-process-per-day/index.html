How Many Boards Can a Six-Sided Drill Process Per Day?

May 10, 2024

The number of boards a six-sided drill can process per day depends on several factors including the size and complexity of the boards, the specific machine model, and operational efficiency.

Key Factors Affecting Production

  • Board Size: Larger boards may take longer to process
  • Drilling Pattern Complexity: More holes and patterns require more time
  • Machine Efficiency: Well-maintained machines perform optimally
  • Operator Skill: Experienced operators can maximize throughput
  • Production Mode: Multi-shift operations increase daily output

Typical Production Rates

Based on standard operating conditions, a CNC six-sided drill can typically process:

  • Efficiency: 1.3 - 1.8 slices per minute
  • Hourly Capacity: 190 - 230 square meters (unfolded area)
  • Daily Output: With 8-hour operation, approximately 1,500 - 1,800 square meters

These figures can vary based on board specifications and machine configuration. Contact us for specific production calculations based on your requirements.
