CNC Six-Sided Drilling Machine C6D

Dual-Side Maximum Efficiency Solution

The C6D is our flagship dual-side drilling machine, capable of processing both sides of the panel simultaneously. This revolutionary design delivers the highest productivity in the industry, perfect for the most demanding production environments.

With simultaneous top and bottom drilling capabilities, the C6D can achieve throughput rates up to 2x higher than single-side machines.
